/ BUYER BEHAVIOR

What’s distinctive about Irish buyers.

Ireland isn't a smaller UK. These are the market traits that most affect how we plan campaigns.

A small market means ad fatigue comes fast

With a population of roughly five million, your target audience can see the same ad many times within weeks. We watch frequency closely and refresh creative more often than we would in larger markets.

Local credibility sells

“Irish-owned”, “based in Cork”, “delivered from Dublin”: local proof carries real weight. If you're genuinely Irish, it belongs in your ads.

Post-Brexit caution with UK sellers

Many Irish shoppers have been hit with customs or VAT charges when ordering from the UK. Make it obvious that you ship from within the EU, price in euro and charge no surprise fees.

Dublin vs everywhere else

A large share of the population and most competition is in the Greater Dublin Area. Rural and regional campaigns often have cheaper clicks but smaller audiences, so we plan them separately.

/ LANGUAGE, CURRENCY & SEASONALITY

Euro pricing, an island geography and Irish dates.

  • Currency & VAT: ad accounts and prices in EUR. Consumer prices should include VAT (23% standard rate in Ireland).
  • Republic vs Northern Ireland: targeting “Ireland” in Meta and Google means the Republic. Northern Ireland is part of the UK, with GBP pricing and UK rules. Cross-border businesses need separate campaigns.
  • Language: English throughout. Irish-English phrasing and local references help; Irish (Gaeilge) touches can work for some brands but are rarely required.
  • School calendar: Irish schools typically return in late August, earlier than in England, so back-to-school campaigns start earlier.

/ PLATFORMS & COMPLIANCE

GDPR, cookies and Irish rules.

Ireland is home to the EU headquarters of Meta, Google and other platforms, which puts its data regulator in the spotlight. Small businesses are held to the same core standards.

GDPR & the DPC

Ireland's Data Protection Commission enforces GDPR. Lead forms need a clear purpose and privacy information; data should be used only as described.

Cookie consent

Ireland's ePrivacy Regulations require consent for non-essential cookies. The DPC's guidance expects rejecting cookies to be as easy as accepting them, with no pre-ticked boxes.

Electronic marketing

Email and SMS marketing to individuals generally needs prior consent, with limited exceptions for existing customers. We build automations to respect opt-outs.

Google Consent Mode v2

Required for using Google's ad personalization and measurement features with EEA users. We configure it alongside your consent banner.

Advertising standards

Ireland has its own self-regulatory advertising code covering misleading claims, pricing and ads aimed at children, separate from the UK's CAP Code.

Platform mix

Google and Meta dominate. TikTok reaches younger audiences; LinkedIn matters for B2B in Dublin; classified sites like DoneDeal are important for some categories.

This is practical marketing guidance, not legal advice. For questions about your specific obligations, speak to a qualified adviser in Ireland.
