/ WHAT'S INCLUDED

Technical, on-page and local, in that order.

There's no point writing content for a site Google can't crawl properly. We fix foundations first, then build on them.

Technical SEO audit

Crawlability, indexing, site speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, broken links, redirects, duplicate content, sitemaps and structured data. Delivered as a prioritized fix list, not a 90-page PDF.

Keyword & intent mapping

Which searches matter for your business, which page should rank for each, and where you're missing pages entirely. One clear target per page.

On-page optimization

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, images and copy improvements on your most important pages, written for people first.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization, categories, services, photos, review strategy, and consistent business details across directories.

Content planning

Service pages, location pages and articles that answer real customer questions. We plan topics around search demand and your expertise, not word counts.

Search Console reporting

Impressions, clicks, rankings for priority terms and the leads organic search sends you, with notes on what changed and why.

/ WHO IT'S FOR

For businesses willing to play the longer game.

SEO takes months, not days. It's a strong fit when you want a channel that keeps working even when ad budgets are tight.

A strong fit if…

  • You have a website you control (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix or custom).
  • You serve a defined area or niche where people search for what you do.
  • You can share expertise for content: answers to common customer questions.
  • You're thinking in quarters, not weeks.
  • You want to reduce long-term dependence on paid ads.

Probably not the right fit if…

  • You need leads this week (start with ads, add SEO alongside).
  • You can't make changes to your website at all.
  • You're looking for guaranteed #1 rankings. Nobody can honestly promise that.
  • You want mass-produced content or link schemes. We don't do either.
