/ WHAT'S INCLUDED

Search intent, done properly.

Google Ads rewards precision. Most wasted budget comes from loose keywords, missing negatives and tracking that counts the wrong things.

Keyword & intent research

We map the searches that signal buying intent in your market, separate them from research-only queries, and estimate what a click is likely to cost before you commit budget.

Conversion tracking via GA4 & Tag Manager

Calls, form submissions, bookings and purchases tracked as conversions, with enhanced conversions where possible. If it isn't measured, Google can't optimize for it.

Ad copy & assets

Responsive search ads written around your actual differentiators, plus sitelinks, callouts, call and location assets that make your ad bigger and more useful.

Campaign types that fit

Search first for most small businesses. Performance Max, Shopping or Demand Gen when there's a feed, enough conversion data, or a clear reason to use them.

Local targeting

Radius and location targeting matched to where you actually serve, ad scheduling around your opening hours, and call tracking for businesses that live on the phone.

Search term hygiene

We read your search terms report regularly and add negative keywords, so budget stops leaking into irrelevant searches. Unglamorous, and where a lot of money is saved.

/ WHO IT'S FOR

Best for businesses people already search for.

If customers type what you sell into Google, there's demand to capture. The question is whether the economics of each click work for your margins.

A strong fit if…

  • Customers search for your service when they need it (“emergency plumber”, “dentist near me”, “private school in…”).
  • You can handle calls and enquiries promptly during the hours your ads run.
  • Your average sale or customer value comfortably covers a realistic cost per lead.
  • You have a website page that clearly explains the offer, or you'll let us improve one.
  • You sell products online and can provide a clean product feed for Shopping.

Probably not the right fit if…

  • Nobody searches for what you sell yet (a brand-new category is usually a Meta job first).
  • Your margins can't absorb competitive click prices in your area.
  • You want to show up for every possible keyword regardless of intent.
  • The landing page can't be changed and doesn't explain the offer.
/ PROCESS

A Google Ads account that gets cleaner over time.

Good Google Ads management is a steady process of tightening: better keywords, better ads, better pages, fewer wasted clicks.

LAUNCH TYPICALLY WITHIN 48 HOURS OF SIGN-OFF
  1. 01

    Audit & research STAGE 01/05

    Account audit if you have one, keyword research and a look at who is bidding in your market. You get a written plan with the campaigns we recommend and why.

  2. 02

    Tracking STAGE 02/05

    GA4, Google Tag Manager and conversion actions configured and tested. Phone calls and forms count; page views don't.

  3. 03

    Build STAGE 03/05

    Tightly themed campaigns and ad groups, ad copy and assets, location and schedule settings, and a starting negative keyword list.

  4. 04

    Optimize STAGE 04/05

    Search term reviews, bid strategy adjustments as conversion data builds, ad copy tests and landing page recommendations.

  5. 05

    Expand STAGE 05/05

    Once the core search campaigns are profitable, we test additional keyword themes, Performance Max or remarketing where it makes sense.
