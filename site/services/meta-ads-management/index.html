/ SERVICE · META ADS

Meta Ads management that pays for itself, not just the platform.

Facebook and Instagram are where most small businesses first try paid ads, and where most of them quietly lose money. We build Meta campaigns around one question: what does a customer cost you, and is that number getting better?

/ WHAT'S INCLUDED

Everything a Meta account needs to actually work.

Not just “boosting posts”. A Meta account that produces predictable results needs tracking, structure, creative and follow-up working together.

Pixel, Conversions API & event setup

We install or audit the Meta Pixel and server-side Conversions API, define the events that matter (lead, booking, purchase) and verify they fire correctly before any money is spent.

Account & campaign architecture

A simple structure the algorithm can learn from: separate prospecting and retargeting, consolidated ad sets, and clear naming so you can read the account yourself.

Creative concepts every month

New angles, hooks and formats for feed, Stories and Reels. Creative is the main lever on Meta now, so we treat it as part of management, not an extra.

Audience strategy

Broad targeting where the algorithm does best, plus custom audiences from your site visitors, video viewers, customer lists and lookalikes where they still earn their place.

Lead form & landing page review

Instant Forms with the right qualifying questions, or a landing page review so paid clicks land somewhere that converts. A cheap lead nobody can reach is not a cheap lead.

Reporting you can read

Cost per lead or purchase, spend, what we tested, what we learned, and what we're doing next. In plain language, every month (or weekly on Dominate).

/ WHO IT'S FOR

Built for businesses that sell to people who scroll.

Meta creates demand. It works best when your offer is visual, easy to understand in a few seconds, and relevant to a definable group of people.

A strong fit if…

  • You sell something people can picture: a treatment, a product, a course, a finished renovation.
  • You can respond to new leads quickly, ideally the same day.
  • You have, or are willing to produce, photos and short videos of the real business.
  • You want to reach people before they start comparing providers on Google.
  • You're ready to give a campaign at least a few weeks of learning before judging it.

Probably not the right fit if…

  • Your customers only buy when an urgent need hits (Google Ads usually fits better first).
  • You want guaranteed results in week one.
  • Your product falls into a category Meta heavily restricts and you can't adjust the messaging.
  • There's no one available to answer the leads that come in.
/ PROCESS

From audit to scaling, in the open.

You always know what's live, what's being tested and why. Nothing changes in the account without a reason we can explain.

LAUNCH TYPICALLY WITHIN 48 HOURS OF SIGN-OFF
  1. 01

    Audit STAGE 01/05

    We review your existing ad account (if any), tracking, offer, landing pages and competitors' active ads in the Meta Ad Library. You get a written summary of what's working and what isn't.

  2. 02

    Tracking STAGE 02/05

    Pixel and Conversions API verified, conversion events prioritized, and lead or purchase data flowing back to Meta so it can optimize toward real outcomes.

  3. 03

    Build STAGE 03/05

    Campaign structure, audiences, first creative batch and copy variations. You approve everything before it goes live.

  4. 04

    Learn STAGE 04/05

    The first weeks are about data. We watch delivery daily, cut what clearly isn't working and avoid knee-jerk edits that reset learning.

  5. 05

    Scale STAGE 05/05

    Winning ads and audiences get more budget in measured steps. New creative batches keep performance from fading as audiences see the same ads.
