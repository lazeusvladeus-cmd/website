/ THE STORY

Why Vike exists.

Most small businesses that try paid advertising hit the same wall. Large agencies are built for large budgets, and small accounts get junior attention. Freelancers can be excellent, but one person rarely covers ads, creative, tracking and automation. And doing it yourself means learning a dozen tools while also running a business.

Vike was set up to sit in the gap: a focused team that treats a small business's budget with the same rigor a large brand would get, at prices a small business can justify. Every account starts with an audit, runs on a documented strategy, and is judged on cost per lead, cost per customer and revenue, not impressions.

We're based in Lviv and work remotely with English-speaking clients worldwide. That keeps our costs reasonable, and our clients get senior attention without paying for an office they'll never visit.

/ HOW WE WORK

Principles we don’t bend.

You own everything

Ad accounts, pixels, analytics, creative. Everything is set up under your ownership. If we part ways, nothing is held hostage.

No guarantees we can't keep

Advertising depends on markets, platforms and offers we don't control. We promise rigorous work and honest reporting, never a specific number of leads.

Tracking before spending

We don't launch campaigns that can't be measured. Conversion tracking is verified before a single dollar is spent.

No markup on ad spend

You pay platforms directly. Our flat fee is our only income from your account, so we have no reason to push spend up.

Plain-English reporting

What we spent, what it produced, what we learned and what we'll do next. Readable in five minutes.

We'll say no

If your margins, capacity or offer make success unlikely, we'll tell you on the first call rather than take your money.
