Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vike Marketing (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal data when you visit vikemarketing.com, contact us, or use our services. We keep it short and specific.

1. Who we are

Vike Marketing is a performance marketing agency based in Lviv, Ukraine. For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at sales@vikemarketing.com or +380 67 674 09 03.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a call, use a contact form or email us, we collect what you provide: usually your name and an email address or phone number, plus anything you choose to share about your business.
  • Client account information. If you become a client, we access your advertising, analytics and related accounts as needed to deliver the services you engage us for.
  • Website usage data. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived on the site.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries, schedule calls and prepare proposals.
  • To deliver, manage and report on the services you engage us for.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it (only with your consent).
  • To meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing.

4. Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on: steps prior to entering a contract when you ask us for a call or proposal; performance of a contract when we deliver services; consent for analytics cookies; legitimate interests in running and securing our business; and legal obligation for records we must keep.

5. How form submissions are delivered

When you submit the booking or contact form, your name, contact details and the page you submitted from are sent to us as a notification through the Telegram messaging service, so we can respond quickly. If that isn't available, your device opens a pre-filled email to sales@vikemarketing.com instead, which you choose whether to send.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful. Analytics cookies are off by default and only set if you click “Accept analytics” in our cookie banner. We use Google Consent Mode, so without your consent Google receives no analytics cookies from your visit. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. We store your choice in your browser's local storage.

Fonts are hosted on our own server, so loading the site does not send your IP address to a font provider.

7. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed: Google (analytics, only with your consent), Telegram (form notifications), our email provider, and our hosting provider Netlify. When we run campaigns for clients, we work inside platforms such as Meta, Google and TikTok under the client's own accounts and the platforms' terms.

8. International transfers

We are based in Ukraine and some of our providers process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where data protection law requires it, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses offered by those providers.

9. How long we keep data

Enquiry details are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact unless you become a client. Client records are kept for the duration of the engagement and as long as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes afterwards. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email sales@vikemarketing.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, for example the ICO in the UK, the Data Protection Commission in Ireland or the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands.

11. Security

We use reputable providers and restrict access to client and enquiry data to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The “last updated” date at the top shows the latest version.
