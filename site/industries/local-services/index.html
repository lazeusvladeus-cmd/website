/ THE REAL PROBLEMS

The problems we see in local businesses.

Dental clinics, salons and gyms face different specifics, but the same patterns come up again and again.

  1. 01

    Leads that never get called back

    Enquiries arrive while staff are with patients or clients. By the time someone calls back, the person has booked elsewhere. Speed to lead often matters more than ad optimization.

  2. 02

    Invisible on Google Maps

    An incomplete Google Business Profile, wrong categories, few recent reviews or no photos keeps you out of the local map results where many bookings start.

  3. 03

    Promotions that attract bargain hunters

    Heavy intro discounts can fill the calendar with people who never return. Offers need to attract clients who fit your pricing long-term.

  4. 04

    Seasonal swings

    Gyms peak around the new year and before summer; salons around holidays and events; dental demand is steadier but high-value treatments need nurturing. Budgets should follow those cycles.

  5. 05

    Restricted ad categories

    Health-related ads face platform policies on claims, before/after imagery and personal attributes. Getting this wrong means rejected ads or a restricted account.

  6. 06

    No idea which ads produce patients

    Without call tracking and booking-system integration, you can see clicks but not which campaigns filled the chair, the table or the class.

/ CHANNELS THAT FIT

Channels that fit local service businesses.

The mix shifts by business type. A dental clinic leans on search; a new gym or salon often leans on Meta.

Google Search & Maps

CORE CHANNEL

Captures “dentist near me”, “emergency dentist”, “hair salon in [area]” searches. Call assets and location assets connect ads to your phone and map listing.

Google Business Profile & local SEO

CORE CHANNEL

Free visibility in the map pack. Categories, services, photos, posts and a steady stream of genuine reviews compound over time.

Meta Ads

CORE FOR GYMS & SALONS

Ideal for visual services and introductory offers: transformation stories (within policy), behind-the-scenes, team intros, trial classes, seasonal packages. Instant Forms or booking links.

Social media management

TRUST BUILDER

People check your Instagram before booking. A consistent, active profile turns ad clicks into bookings.

Automation (SMS / email / WhatsApp)

MULTIPLIER

Instant replies to new leads, appointment reminders and re-booking prompts reduce no-shows and bring clients back.

TikTok

SITUATIONAL

Can work for salons and gyms with a personality-driven brand and staff comfortable on camera.

/ EXAMPLE CAMPAIGN STRUCTURES

Example setups for three local businesses.

These are illustrative frameworks showing how we'd typically organize an account, not results from a specific client. Your actual structure depends on budget, offer and market.

Dental clinic: search-led

Separating urgent demand from high-value elective treatments, because they behave very differently.

  • Google Search: Emergency & general
    • Emergency dentist / toothache / near me
    • Call assets during opening hours (ads scheduled to staffed hours)
  • Google Search: High-value treatments
    • Implants, Invisalign/aligners, whitening as separate ad groups
    • Landing pages per treatment with pricing guidance and FAQs
  • Meta: Treatment awareness + retargeting
    • Educational video on a treatment, consultation offer
    • Retarget site visitors (policy-compliant, no personal-attribute claims)

Salon or gym: Meta-led

Visual offers and a fast booking path, supported by local search for people ready to book now.

  • Meta: Intro offer (lead or booking objective)
    • Radius around the location, broad age band
    • Creative: real space, real staff, real clients; Reels first
    • Instant Form with 2–3 qualifying questions
  • Meta: Retargeting
    • Video viewers + Instagram engagers + site visitors
    • Testimonials, class schedule, booking link
  • Google: Local search
    • “Gym near me”, “[treatment] in [area]”
    • Location assets linked to Google Business Profile
  • Automation
    • Instant SMS/WhatsApp reply to new leads
    • Reminder 24h before first visit
