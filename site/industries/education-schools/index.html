/ THE REAL PROBLEMS

What makes education marketing different.

We've worked with a junior school in Lviv, and the same dynamics show up across private schools, language schools and course providers.

  1. 01

    Enrollment windows are short

    Most decisions cluster around specific months before the school year or term starts. Campaigns that start late miss the window entirely.

  2. 02

    The buyer isn't the student

    For schools, parents decide. For adult courses, the learner decides but may need employer or partner buy-in. Messaging has to speak to the person making the call.

  3. 03

    Trust before anything else

    Parents want to see teachers, classrooms, the atmosphere and other families' experiences. Polished stock imagery undermines that trust.

  4. 04

    Enquiries that go cold

    A parent requests information, gets one email and never returns. Without a nurture sequence and a clear next step (open day, trial lesson, call) leads leak.

  5. 05

    Special rules for minors

    Platforms restrict targeting of under-18s and some education claims. Campaigns should target parents and adult learners, not children.

  6. 06

    Word of mouth that can't scale

    Most good schools grow through referrals, which is great but slow. Ads and SEO help reach families who haven't heard of you yet.

/ CHANNELS THAT FIT

Channels that fit schools and course providers.

Parents search and scroll. The best education campaigns use both, timed to the calendar.

Meta Ads

CORE CHANNEL

Reach parents in your area with real school life: classroom video, teacher intros, open day invitations. Lead forms or event registrations as the conversion.

Google Search

CORE CHANNEL

“Private school [city]”, “English course for kids”, “IELTS preparation online”. High intent, especially in the weeks before enrollment deadlines.

SEO & Google Business Profile

LONG-TERM

Program pages, admissions FAQ and a well-reviewed Business Profile rank year-round and support every paid campaign.

Social media management

TRUST BUILDER

An active Instagram or Facebook page showing everyday school life is often what convinces a parent to book a visit.

Email & messenger nurture

ESSENTIAL

Follow-up sequences after an enquiry: what to expect, fees, FAQs, open day reminders, application deadlines.

YouTube

SUPPORTING

Longer tours and teacher interviews for families who are seriously considering you.

/ EXAMPLE CAMPAIGN STRUCTURES

Example setups for education businesses.

These are illustrative frameworks showing how we'd typically organize an account, not results from a specific client. Your actual structure depends on budget, offer and market.

Private school: enrollment season

Three phases timed to the admissions calendar, each with its own goal.

  • Phase 1: Awareness (8–12 weeks before deadline)
    • Meta video: day-in-the-life, teachers, facilities
    • Parents within commuting radius; exclude current families
  • Phase 2: Open day / tour registrations
    • Meta event or lead form campaign
    • Google Search: school name, “private school [city]”, curriculum terms
  • Phase 3: Application push
    • Retarget registrants and site visitors
    • Deadline reminders via email and messenger

Course or tutoring provider: always-on

For providers with rolling intakes or online delivery, an always-on structure with seasonal boosts.

  • Google Search: Course-specific ad groups
    • Exam prep, language level, subject + “online” / city
    • Landing page per course with schedule and pricing
  • Meta: Free trial lesson or level test
    • Lead form with goal, level and preferred schedule
    • Creative: real teacher on camera explaining the method
  • Automation
    • Instant confirmation + calendar link for trial
    • 3-step follow-up if the trial isn't booked
